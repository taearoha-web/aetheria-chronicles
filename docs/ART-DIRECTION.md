# Character roster

The owner approved beasts and spirits as playable heroes on 2026-10-08 and requested that hero appearances never duplicate each other. All eight hero IDs keep their purchase prices, statistics, skills and saved progression. Their models are now separate user-provided GLBs.

There are three distinct normal enemy species (tiger, pegasus and peacock) and five chapter-specific boss models. Every encounter uses a different model for each simultaneous enemy. The manifest records exact source paths and named skeletal clips. All new derivatives retain rigs and combat clips, use approximately 18,000 triangles, and cap textures at 512px. The starting knight and healer retain the previous assets. Total active GLBs are approximately 23.2 MiB; initial boot loads only the starting duo. Additional characters are cached when selected or encountered.

All original assets remain untouched. Detailed silhouettes and recognizable designs come from the owner's existing project. Art styles are mixed and should not be described as final King's Raid-quality human hero production. Future work can replace individual heroes while preserving their IDs and saves.

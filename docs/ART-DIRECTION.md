# Character roster

The owner approved beasts and spirits as playable heroes on 2026-10-08 and requested that hero appearances never duplicate each other. The owner subsequently supplied a human knight and priestess as the starting duo. All eight hero IDs keep their purchase prices and saved progression. The starter skill presentation and kits are documented in STARTER-HEROES.md.

There are three distinct normal enemy species (tiger, pegasus and peacock) and five chapter-specific boss models. Every encounter uses a different model for each simultaneous enemy. The manifest records source provenance and named skeletal clips. Most supporting creatures use approximately 18,000 triangles and 512px textures. The new starting duo have their own Blender-authored rigs, ten named animation clips, and larger texture/detail budgets to preserve their faces. See the generated asset validation report for actual counts and sizes. Initial boot loads only the starting duo; additional characters are cached when selected or encountered.

All original assets remain untouched. Detailed silhouettes and recognizable designs come from the owner's existing project. Art styles are mixed and should not be described as final King's Raid-quality human hero production. Future work can replace individual heroes while preserving their IDs and saves.

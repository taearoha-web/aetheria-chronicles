# Active character asset provenance

All 16 active rigged, textured character models are from the owner's existing Seven Elements project. The owner authorized their use and approved playable beasts and spirits. These proprietary assets retain their original provider license and owner rights; they are not relicensed as CC0 or covered by the source-code license. No new paid asset generation was used.

New derivatives were optimized in Blender to approximately 18,000 triangles and embedded JPEG textures capped at 512px, while preserving skeletons and named animations. Validation records final counts and SHA-256 hashes. Hero portraits are current renders of the shipped models or matching portraits reused from the owner project (Elias, Nova and Lucien). The original owner's files were not modified.

Legacy mage.glb, rogue.glb and enemy.glb are no longer referenced by the runtime manifest. The legacy rogue and goblin files came from AutoSprite under CC0-1.0; their unused files retain that original provenance. No King's Raid extracted assets are used.
